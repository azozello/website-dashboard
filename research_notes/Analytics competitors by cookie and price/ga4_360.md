# Google Analytics 360 (GA4 360): what the paid enterprise tier adds over standard GA4 (as of November 2026)

Scope: only the **differences** from free GA4. The standard GA4 catalog is in `research_notes/GA4 and Plausible metrics and features/ga4_features.md` and `ga4_metrics_dimensions.md`.

Abbreviations:
- "360 page" = [[GA4] Google Analytics 360](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11202874?hl=en), Google's official standard-vs-360 table.
- "SLA doc" = [GA 360 SLA for GA4 Properties, last updated Dec 15, 2022](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics_products/sla/).
- "R25" = [What's new archive](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/13420269?hl=en).
- "R26" = [What's new 2026](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9164320?hl=en).

## 1. Limits: standard vs 360

### Takeaway
360 raises almost every per-property quota by roughly 2.5× to 10×:
- Custom definitions: 50 → 125. Parameters: 25 → 100. Audiences: 100 → 400. Calculated metrics: 5 → 50.
- Explore sampling threshold: 10M → 1B events per query.
- Data API: 200K → 2M tokens per day.
- Retention: 14 → 50 months.
- BigQuery daily export: 1M events/day → billions.

Some limits are **identical** in both tiers: explorations per user (200), manual data imports (120/day), web event-name cardinality (unlimited), streaming BigQuery export (unlimited), and Chat tokens.

- **Custom channel groups**: 2 (standard) vs 5 (360), per third parties only (carried over from the GA4 notes) — [KP Playbook](https://kpplaybook.com/resources/ga4-new-channel-groups/)

### Inferences
- The limits Google's table doesn't vary are a useful contrast point. 360 does **not** lift web event-name cardinality, Explore's per-user exploration count, manual import frequency, or streaming export (already unlimited on standard). Its gains are all in volume, quota and retention.

### Gaps
- The official 360 page rendered with a partly garbled table, so two rows are uncertain: event-parameter value length and the Large/XL retention restriction.
- No official number found for properties per account on standard GA4, for audience-trigger limits, or for custom channel groups (only third-party figures).

## 2. 360-only features, service levels and integrations

### Takeaway
What 360 adds beyond bigger limits:
- **Structural**: subproperties and roll-up properties.
- **Data access**: expanded data sets, unsampled explorations and BigQuery Fresh Daily export.
- **Freshness**: about 1h intraday data, vs 2–6h on standard.
- **Contractual SLAs**: 99.9% collection, 99% reporting, and processing within 4h for Normal properties. Credits are capped at 25% of the monthly fee.
- **Admin, governance and support**: enterprise support.

The SA360, DV360 and CM360/Floodlight integrations are **not** 360-only in GA4 (they were in Universal Analytics). 360 only extends them to subproperties and roll-ups. No new 360-only features appear in the 2026 release notes. The last 360-specific release note was GA360 property syncing (Jun 2, 2025).

### Cited Findings

**Property structure (360-only)**
- **Subproperties**: a property fed by a source 360 property, usually a filtered subset of its events. Used to govern user access and filter data.
  - Limits: up to 400 per source property. They count toward 2,000 properties per account.
  - Restrictions: no own data streams, no event creation or modification, no offline event import. Audience triggers are supported. Custom definitions aren't inherited unless syncing is turned on.
  — [Subproperties](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11525732?hl=en)
- **Roll-up properties**: aggregate data from up to 200 source 360 properties into one data set, for example across brands or regions.
  - A roll-up collects no data itself and can't feed another roll-up.
  - No subproperties can be created from a roll-up, and roll-ups can't be downgraded.
  - Data accumulates from the date each source is added.
  - The roll-up needs its own product links, and Google signals must be enabled on the roll-up itself.
  — [Roll-ups](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11526039?hl=en); [360 page](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11202874?hl=en)
- **GA360 property syncing** (Jun 2, 2025): syncs custom dimensions and metrics from a source property to new or empty subproperties — [R25](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/13420269?hl=en)

**Data access and completeness (360-only)**
- **Unsampled explorations**: Analysts can request unsampled Explore results within 20K tokens/day (5K max per query). These are processed outside the SLA in about 1h — [360 page](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11202874?hl=en); [Non-standard processing](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10742670?hl=en)
- **Expanded data sets**: pre-defined dimension/metric sets that expand the "(other)" row of reports and explorations.
  - Expand up to 2M rows; 100 per property.
  - Take 48h after the request and backfill 52 days.
  - Processing time is 24h, outside the SLA.
  - Not available for Advertising reports. Queryable via the API and Looker Studio.
  — [Expanded data sets (search snippet)](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/12867885?hl=en); [Expanded data timing (search snippet)](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/14170427); [Non-standard processing](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10742670?hl=en)
- **BigQuery Fresh Daily export**: 360 Normal and Large properties only, not XL.
  - Delivery: starts shortly after midnight, typically completes by ~5am property time, then updates hourly.
  - Same schema as Daily, including post-collection enrichment (ad attribution, geo/device).
  - Requires GCP billing.
  - Its help page calls it "stable and generally available"; the Data freshness page still labels it "beta" (conflict).
  — [Fresh Daily](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/15604391?hl=en); [Data freshness](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11198161?hl=en)
- **Higher BigQuery Daily export cap**: "billions" of events/day vs 1M on standard — [360 page](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11202874?hl=en)

**Data freshness**
- **Intraday**: about **1 hour** on 360 (Premium Normal and Large) vs **2–6 hours** on standard. The "~1h vs 4–8h" figure in the brief is third-party. Google's own page says 2–6h; 4–8h is the official *attribution* processing time — [Data freshness](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11198161?hl=en); [Non-standard processing](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10742670?hl=en)
- **Daily**: 12h (standard and 360 Normal), 18h (360 Large), 24h+ (360 XL).
  - Size tiers: Normal is under 25B events per 31 days; Large is 25B or more; XL is 250B or more.
  - Google: the timeline "isn't a guarantee, nor an SLA or an SLO."
  — [Data freshness](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11198161?hl=en)

**Service Level Agreements (360-only)**
Source: [SLA doc](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics_products/sla/).

| SLA component | Target | Credit if missed |
|---|---|---|
| **Collection SLA** | 99.9% uptime | 5% (96–<99.9%), 10% (93–<96%), 15% (90–<93%), 25% (<90%) |
| **Reporting SLA** | 99% uptime (UI login and reporting requests). Not applicable to XL properties | same bands from <99% |
| **Data Processing SLA** | 98% of the time: Normal within **4h of receipt**; Large within **48h** of midnight PT; XL within **7 days** of midnight PT | same bands from <98% |

- Maximum credit is 25% of the month's Analytics 360 fees.
- **Termination right**: if SLAs are missed 3 months in a row, or 4 times in 12 months.
- Claims must be filed through Google technical support within 30 days.
- **SLA exclusions**:
  - Integration features (Google Ads, AdSense, BigQuery, Firebase), beta/alpha features and UA properties. Customer-initiated upgrades are also excluded.
  - Coverage starts only once the account is on a 360 contract for GA4.
  — [SLA doc](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics_products/sla/); [360 page](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11202874?hl=en)
- **Features with non-standard processing** (outside the processing SLA): Realtime <1 min; unsampled exploration 1h; attribution 4–8h; user-lifetime 24h; Insights 24h; expanded data sets 24h; BigQuery daily export 24h after midnight; Data Import 24–48h — [Non-standard processing](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10742670?hl=en)
- **Not covered by the Reporting SLA** (best effort): Realtime, Data API queries, Audience List API queries — [Reporting SLA exceptions (search snippet)](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10999787)
- **BigQuery Export SLA**: applies only to 360 Normal/Large properties that select Fresh Daily, but the page states the Fresh Daily SLA "isn't currently available."
  - This conflicts with Google's marketing page, which says SLAs cover "data collection through attribution, and BigQuery daily export."
  — [BigQuery Export SLA](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/14117050?hl=en); [Analytics 360 product page](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics-360/)

**Admin, governance and support (360-specific)**
- **Self-service upgrade/downgrade**: done by Org or Billing admins; requires a Google Marketing Platform organization with an active 360 order. Upgrades take up to 4h. Partner-sold accounts must be changed by the partner — [360 page](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11202874?hl=en)
- **GMP Admin API** (Mar 8, 2024): programmatic upgrade/downgrade between standard and 360, and linking accounts to organizations — [R25](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/13420269?hl=en)
- **Report collection assignments** (May 14, 2024): 360 admins assign users or groups to specific report collections — [R25](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/13420269?hl=en)
- **GMP groups in scheduled emails** (Jun 20, 2024): 360 admins can send report emails to Google Marketing Platform groups — [R25](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/13420269?hl=en)
- **Data access and API quota history** (Nov 7, 2024): access and quota logs, built on GA360 Access Logs — [R25](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/13420269?hl=en)
- **Bill Preview**: lets Google Analytics Certified Partners estimate GA4 360 costs — [Account structure](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9679158?hl=en)
- **Enterprise support**: "enterprise-level support" and SLAs are named as 360 benefits; partners can set service levels for their clients — [Analytics 360 product page](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics-360/); [360 page](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11202874?hl=en)
- **Reseller support** (2017 Americas terms): resellers provide implementation guidance and front-end technical support and may charge beyond 10 hours/month. Google provides webinar training — [Customer Minimum Terms](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/reseller-cmt/na/)

**Google Marketing Platform integrations: are they 360-only?**
- **Display & Video 360 link**: available to both standard and 360 GA4 properties since launch (2022). Up to 200 advertisers — [Louder](https://louder.com.au/2022/03/08/dv360-and-ga4-integration); [DV360 link help](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10439659)
- **Search Ads 360 link**: "no longer only available to GA360" as it was in UA; works on standard GA4 — [Louder](https://louder.com.au/2022/03/30/search-ads-360-and-google-analytics-4-integration); [SA360 help](https://support.google.com/sa360/answer/12980043)
- **Campaign Manager 360 / Floodlight link**: exports GA4 key events to Floodlight for CM360/DV360/SA360 bidding and imports CM360 cost data. Google's prerequisites (property collecting data, at least one key event, Floodlight configuration linked) don't mention 360 — [GA4 Floodlight help (search snippet)](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/14615126); [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/ga4-strengthens-campaign-measurement-with-floodlight-linking)
- **360-specific extensions**: SA360 and DV360 linking for subproperties and roll-ups (Feb 7, 2024); Floodlight linking for subproperties and roll-ups (Jun 3, 2024). SA360 subproperty/roll-up data is inherited from the source property — [R25](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/13420269?hl=en); [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/ga4-launch-subproperties-and-roll-up-properties-integration-with-search-ads-360-and-display-video-360); [Account structure](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9679158?hl=en)
- Google's 360 product page lists integrations with Google Ads, SA360, DV360, Google Cloud, Search Console and Salesforce, with no claim that they are exclusive — [Analytics 360 product page](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics-360/)

**2025–2026 360-only additions**
- 2025: **GA360 property syncing** (Jun 2, 2025) is the only 360-specific release note — [R25](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/13420269?hl=en)
- 2026: the release notes (Jan 16–Nov 6, 2026) contain **no** 360-specific items — [R26](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9164320?hl=en)

### Inferences
- Several 360 selling points from the UA era are no longer differentiators in GA4:
  - Standard GA4 reports are not sampled.
  - SA360, DV360 and CM360 links are open to standard properties.
  - Streaming BigQuery export is available to everyone.
- What remains uniquely 360 is scale (quotas, 1B Explore sampling threshold, unsampled tokens, 50-month retention), multi-property structure (subproperties, roll-ups), faster or more complete data (1h intraday, Fresh Daily, expanded data sets), and contractual SLAs and support.
- The SLA is narrower than the marketing suggests. BigQuery and other integration features are excluded, the Fresh Daily SLA is "not currently available," and Reporting SLA doesn't cover XL properties or API queries.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found that 360 offers more **audience triggers** or more/better **conversion modeling** than standard. No source mentions such differences.
- No current "custom/advanced reporting" feature exclusive to GA4 360 was found. UA-era Custom Tables have no GA4 equivalent in the sources checked.
- The fetched SLA doc is dated Dec 15, 2022 and links to "previous versions". No newer version was confirmed.
- The exact launch date of Fresh Daily GA was not found.

## 3. Pricing and how it is sold

### Takeaway
Google publishes **no price list**. 360 is sold by quote, on an **annual contract** (12-month minimum, auto-renewing yearly), priced by **billable event volume**.
- Events in subproperties and roll-ups bill at an extra 0.5× each, per Google.
- Third-party estimates (2022–2026) put the entry price at about **$50,000/year for up to 25M events/month**, rising to **$150K+/year** around 500M events/month.
- It is sold through Google sales ("Talk to Sales") or Google Marketing Platform Partners (certified resellers).

### Cited Findings
- **No public price**: Google's 360 page offers "Talk to Sales" and links to Google Marketing Platform Partners. No price is shown — [Analytics 360 product page](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics-360/); [Embertribe, 2026](https://embertribe.com/blog/google-analytics-cost)
- **Contract-based billing**: 360 is sold through contracts for GA4 properties. Upgrading a property doesn't change billing until the account moves onto a 360 contract. Sales Partners can set service levels — [360 page](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11202874?hl=en)
- **Billable events in subproperties and roll-ups**: each event sent to a subproperty or roll-up is processed again. Each such hit "costs 0.5 of an event hit" — [Account structure](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9679158?hl=en); [GMP Academy deck (older)](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/gmp_academy_new_google_analytics_4_paradigm.pdf)
  - Third-party worked example: 100 events copied to 1 subproperty bill as 150. One source claims events filtered out of a subproperty aren't charged (unverified) — [userinsight.ai](https://userinsight.ai/google-analytics-360-pricing)
- **Third-party price estimates** (all labeled estimates, none from Google):
  - **$50,000/year retail start for 25M events/month**: Cardinal Path (a 360 reseller), Aug 2022, as reported by Rybbit, Sep 13, 2026 — [Rybbit](https://rybbit.com/blog/google-analytics-pricing)
  - **$4,200/month base for 25M events plus $17.50 per additional million events**: from the Vision Labs GA360 cost calculator (Google Cloud partner; undated, checked Sep 2026) — [Rybbit](https://rybbit.com/blog/google-analytics-pricing)
  - **Typical $50,000 to $150,000+/year** (Vision Labs; Embertribe "2026 market data"). Embertribe ties the ~$150K+ figure to ~500M monthly hits — [Rybbit](https://rybbit.com/blog/google-analytics-pricing); [Embertribe](https://embertribe.com/blog/google-analytics-cost)
  - "Likely to cost you $150k or more per year", billed monthly on billable events under a 360 contract — [OptimizeSmart](https://optimizesmart.com/blog/ga4-vs-ga4-360-pricing-limits-billing-and-more/)
  - **Legacy UA 360**: $150,000/year for up to 500M hits/month, plus a suggested $30–50K implementation (InfoTrust, Apr 2020). This is the source of the common "$150K" figure and is not GA4 pricing — [Rybbit](https://rybbit.com/blog/google-analytics-pricing)
- **Minimum term**:
  - InfoTrust: licenses are annual, Google offers no short-term contracts, and licenses must start on the 1st of a month. It advises against partners quoting an exact price upfront, because cost depends on traffic, support needs, and the number of sites/apps — [InfoTrust (updated Feb 17, 2025)](https://infotrust.com/articles/ga360-insights-and-recommendations/)
  - Google's reseller Customer Minimum Terms (Americas, dated Apr 1, 2017, "Analytics Premium" era): initial term set in the reseller's purchase order, auto-renewal "for additional one-year periods" unless notice is given at least 30 days before expiry, and a cap of 20B hits/month without Google approval (roll-up hits = 0.5) — [Customer Minimum Terms](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/reseller-cmt/na/)
- **Who can change tiers**: partner-sold accounts can only be upgraded or downgraded by the Sales Partner — [360 page](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11202874?hl=en)
- **Extra costs on top of the license**:
  - Fresh Daily and streaming BigQuery export need GCP billing; storage is billed — [Fresh Daily](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/15604391?hl=en)
  - Implementation is usually a separate agency engagement — [Rybbit](https://rybbit.com/blog/google-analytics-pricing)

### Inferences
- Applying the Vision Labs third-party calculator (an inference, not a quote):

| Monthly events | Approx. annual cost |
|---|---|
| 25M | ~$50K |
| 100M | ~$66K |
| 500M | ~$150K |
| 1B | ~$255K |

  This matches the commonly cited $50K–$150K+ band.
- Because subproperty and roll-up events bill at an extra 0.5×, multi-brand setups that duplicate data into roll-ups or subproperties can raise cost materially above the raw event count.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 primary (Google or partner) price sheet was found. All dollar figures are third-party and some trace back to 2022.
- No current (post-2017) Google Customer Minimum Terms were found; the minimum-term statement relies on InfoTrust (2025) plus the 2017 document.

## 4. Who buys it and why, and whether a small business would need it

### Takeaway
Buyers are large enterprises:
- multi-brand or multi-region groups (roll-ups and subproperties),
- high-traffic ecommerce (Explore sampling and BigQuery daily caps),
- data-warehouse-driven teams (Fresh Daily, 2M API tokens),
- organizations that need contractual SLAs and vendor support.

A small business site never approaches the standard limits, so 360's only plausible draw would be longer retention, against a ~$50K/yr floor.

### Cited Findings
- Google pitches 360 as "tailored for the unique measurement needs of large enterprises," with advanced customization, scalable tools, enterprise-level support and SLAs. Its example customer is Rituals (a retailer) — [Analytics 360 product page](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics-360/)
- Embertribe: worth it for large high-traffic ecommerce, real-time warehouse streaming, or unsampled executive dashboards. Free GA4 suits brands under ~500M monthly events whose needs are met by standard Explorations — [Embertribe](https://embertribe.com/blog/google-analytics-cost)
- InfoTrust: reasons cited are unsampled data, more custom definitions, and reseller support for implementation. A good reseller should help decide "whether the enterprise license is needed in the first place" — [InfoTrust](https://infotrust.com/articles/ga360-insights-and-recommendations/)
- Subproperties exist to govern user access and filter data by brand or region; roll-ups aggregate across brands and regions — [Subproperties](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11525732?hl=en); [Roll-ups](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11526039?hl=en)

### Inferences
- For Rate 1's target (small EU business sites, typically thousands to low millions of events per month), every 360 limit is irrelevant:
  - Explore sampling starts at 10M events per query on standard.
  - BigQuery daily export allows 1M events/day on standard.
  - The Data API allows 200K tokens/day on standard.
- The entry price (~$50K/yr, third-party) is several orders of magnitude above small-business analytics budgets.
- In the comparison, GA4 360 is the "enterprise ceiling" of the cookie-based category, not a realistic option for small business.

### Gaps
- No survey or market data was found on 360's customer count or size distribution.

## 5. Privacy and compliance differences

### Takeaway
360 uses the **same tag, cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_<id>`), Consent Mode v2 requirements and EU data handling** as standard GA4. No 360-only privacy or cookieless feature exists in the sources checked. The differences are contractual:
- EEA/UK/CH 360 customers have Google's data processing terms built into their agreement.
- Partner-bought customers need DP terms with Google or the partner.
- 360 allows longer retention (up to 50 months) and adds subproperty-based access governance.

### Cited Findings
- **Data processing terms**:
  - 360 customers based in the EEA, UK or Switzerland already have the data processing terms in their agreements; customers elsewhere accept them in Account Settings.
  - 360 sales partners, and clients who bought through a partner, can't accept the terms in the UI and need separate DP terms with Google or their partner.
  — [Data Processing Terms help](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/3379636)
- **Service Specific Terms**: the Analytics Service Specific Terms (2019, 2021 versions) make Analytics use subject to the Google Ads Data Processing Terms — [GMP terms 2021](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/platforms/gmp/ads_analytics/emea-20210304/)
- **Reseller terms (2017)**:
  - Customer owns Customer Data; Google may not share non-aggregated data with third parties without consent.
  - Customers must not send personally identifying information.
  - Customers must disclose cookie use in a privacy policy.
  — [Customer Minimum Terms](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/reseller-cmt/na/)
- **Longer retention**: 26/38/50-month options on 360 vs 14 months max on standard — [360 page](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11202874?hl=en)
- **Access governance**: subproperties can be used to restrict which users see which data — [Subproperties](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11525732?hl=en)
- **Deletion requests**: a deletion request on a source property also deletes that data from its roll-ups; a request on a roll-up affects only the roll-up — [Roll-ups](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11526039?hl=en)

### Inferences
- Google's 360 documentation lists differences only in limits, SLAs, structure and billing, never in collection method. So the GDPR/ePrivacy analysis in `ga4_features.md` §10 applies unchanged to 360:
  - A consent banner is needed in the EU.
  - Data is lost or modeled when users decline.
  - Processing runs on US-headquartered infrastructure under the DPF.
